# HR-Analytics-Project– Employee Income Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores employee demographics, income distribution, work arrangements, and the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the workplace.

The goal was to analyse employee data, identify patterns in income across different employee groups, and present meaningful insights that can support informed business decision-making.

## Dataset Overview

* Total Records: 5,000 employees
* Total Columns: 29
* Data Category: Human Resources (HR)
* Tool Used: Microsoft Excel

The dataset contains information relating to employee demographics, job roles, monthly income, work experience, performance, engagement, work arrangements, AI adoption, and attrition.

## Project Objectives

* Examine employee income distribution across different demographic groups.
* Compare average monthly income by gender and marital status.
* Explore income differences across remote, hybrid, and on-site work arrangements.
* Investigate the relationship between AI tool usage and employee income.
* Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

The following steps were carried out using Microsoft Excel:

1. Checked for duplicate records. No duplicates were identified.
2. Checked for missing values. No missing values were identified.
3. Standardised the date format.
4. Verified numerical columns to ensure appropriate number formats.
5. Grouped employee ages into categories for easier analysis.
6. Standardised gender categories.
7. Formatted monthly income as currency.
8. Grouped years at the company into tenure categories:
    * Less than 5 years
    * 5–9 years
    * 10 years and above.

## Business Questions

1. What is the total number of employees in the dataset?
2. What are the minimum and maximum employee ages?
3. How does average monthly income differ across genders?
4. How does marital status influence income comparisons across genders?
5. How does work mode relate to average monthly income across genders?
6. Is there a difference in average income between employees who use AI tools at work across genders?

## Key Findings

* The dataset contains 5,000 employees, with employee ages ranging from 21 to 64.
* Average monthly income was approximately $7,000 for females, $6,979 for males, and $6,990 for employees classified as others.
* Income comparisons across marital status showed different patterns depending on gender.
* Among remote employees, males recorded the highest average income.
* Among on-site employees, employees classified as others recorded the highest average income.
* Among hybrid employees, females recorded the highest average income.
* Male employees who use AI tools recorded a higher average income than female employees and employees classified as others.

## Conclusion

The analysis revealed variations in average employee income across gender, marital status, and work arrangements.

One important observation was that no single gender consistently recorded the highest average income across all categories.

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used to clean, organise, analyse, and visualise HR data to generate insights for decision-making.
